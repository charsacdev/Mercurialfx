Leaderboard Rules

Last updated Sep 6th, 2025

The leaderboard is a recognition feature. It does not change your rate, your limits or the order in which your transactions are processed. Nothing here is an inducement to transact more than you intend to — please read section 9 before you compete.
1

The weekly season

A season runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59, West Africa Time (WAT, UTC+1). Every board resets to zero at the start of a new season — scores never carry over.

Final standings are locked 24 hours after a season closes. That delay exists so that transactions still settling on Sunday night, and any reversals, are reflected before winners are confirmed.

2

The five boards

Each board ranks independently. You can appear on all five at once.

Board What it measures
Deposits · count Number of confirmed deposits. Size is irrelevant — a ₦5,000 deposit scores the same as a ₦5,000,000 one.
Deposits · volume Total naira value of confirmed deposits that cleared the settlement window in section 4.
Withdrawals · count Number of payouts completed to your bank account or wallet.
Withdrawals · volume Total naira value paid out to you and confirmed received.
Referrals Referred members who completed KYC and made at least one confirmed transaction. See section 6.

Ties are broken by whoever reached the score first. If both boards and timestamps tie, the higher position goes to the member with the longer account history.

3

Only confirmed transactions score

A transaction scores when our team marks it Confirmed — not when you submit it, and not when you click I have paid. Pending and rejected transactions are worth zero.

If a confirmed transaction is later reversed, charged back or found to be fraudulent, the score is removed and the board is recalculated. This can move you down after the fact, including after a season has closed but before standings lock.

4

The 48-hour settlement window

A deposit scores on the volume boards only if the funds remain in your account for at least 48 hours. Money deposited and immediately withdrawn does not count toward deposit volume.

This exists so that nobody can cycle the same ₦1,000,000 in and out repeatedly to climb both the deposit and withdrawal boards on money they never actually committed. Rapid in-and-out movement of identical amounts is also a pattern we are required to monitor for other reasons — see section 8.

5

Weekly scoring cap

Each member can score a maximum of 60 transactions per season across all boards combined. The cap keeps the count boards a contest of consistency rather than one of raw automation, and it limits how far any single member can run away with a season.

6

What counts as a qualified referral

A referral scores only when the person you invited has:

  • signed up using your referral link or code;
  • completed KYC verification and been approved; and
  • completed at least one confirmed transaction of their own.

Signups alone are worth nothing. Referrals sharing a device, bank account, ID document or payout destination with the referrer are treated as self-referrals, score zero, and are grounds for removal under section 8.

7

Your handle and your privacy

You appear on every public board under a pseudonymous handle. We never publish your legal name, email, phone number, account number, bank or location alongside a transaction amount.

Handles must not impersonate Mercurial staff or another member, contain contact details, or include offensive content. We reserve the right to reset a handle that breaks these rules, and reserved words such as admin, support and mercurial are blocked at signup.

Our compliance team can resolve a handle to an account where required for verification, fraud investigation, payout of a prize, or a lawful request. Every such lookup is logged against the staff member who performed it. Handles are visible to the public — choose one that does not identify you, and treat anything you share elsewhere linking your handle to your identity as your own disclosure.

8

Fair play

The following will remove you from a board, forfeit any prize, and may suspend your account:

  • Operating more than one account, or transacting through another member's account.
  • Cycling the same funds to inflate count or volume (see section 4).
  • Splitting a single intended transaction into many smaller ones purely to raise a count.
  • Creating referral accounts you control, or paying people to sign up without genuine use.
  • Submitting false payment proof or a transaction hash that is not yours.
  • Any attempt to automate transactions against the platform.
9

Please compete responsibly

Rankings reflect payment activity on Mercurial. They are not a measure of trading skill, and a high position is not evidence that anyone is profitable — funds you move through this platform are traded elsewhere, at your own risk. Never deposit money you cannot afford to lose in order to climb a board. If competing is affecting how much you deposit, contact support and we will remove you from the boards permanently on request.

10

Prizes and changes

Prizes for each season are announced in-app before the season opens. Winners are notified through the platform and must have a verified KYC status and a matching bank account to be paid. Prizes are not transferable.

We may amend these rules, adjust the boards, or pause a season. Material changes are announced before they take effect and never applied retroactively to a season already in progress. These rules sit alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
